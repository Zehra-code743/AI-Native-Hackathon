This chapter establishes the *robotic nervous system*—the communication and control backbone that lets intelligent robots sense, decide, and act reliably. Drawing from 2025 research on ROS 2 architecture, real-time performance, high-performance middleware, AI-driven localization, and wireless optimization, we focus on practical patterns that keep distributed robots predictable and efficient.

### Learning goals

- Understand how ROS 2's DDS-based graph connects nodes, topics, services, actions, and parameters for modular systems.
- Configure QoS and scheduling for predictable, real-time behavior in wireless and embedded deployments.
- Apply middleware and profiling techniques that expose bottlenecks before they become failures.
- Strengthen localization and navigation stacks with AI-driven covariance tuning and robust data fusion.
- Build resilient communication for field robots where bandwidth and latency fluctuate.

### What’s inside

- Core architecture of ROS 2 communication and why DDS matters.
- QoS, executors, and task rate regulation for real-time responsiveness.
- Middleware building blocks that support autonomy at scale.
- Localization and navigation pipelines that stay stable under uncertainty.
- Wireless optimization and dataflow profiling to keep robots reliable in the wild.
